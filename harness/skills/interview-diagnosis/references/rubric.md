# Scoring Rubric

## Content Dimensions

### concept_accuracy (0-10)
- 10: Concepts are perfectly accurate with nuanced understanding
- 7-9: Core concepts correct, minor detail gaps
- 4-6: Partial understanding, some conceptual errors
- 1-3: Major conceptual errors or fundamental misunderstandings
- 0: Completely incorrect

### structure_completeness (0-10)
- 10: Well-organized with clear opening, body, and conclusion; all key points covered
- 7-9: Good structure, most key points covered
- 4-6: Some structure but missing important points or jumps between topics
- 1-3: Disorganized, missing major points
- 0: No discernible structure

### engineering_depth (0-10)
- 10: Deep engineering insight, mentions specific tools, trade-offs, and production considerations
- 7-9: Good engineering understanding, mentions some practical details
- 4-6: Surface-level understanding, lacks depth
- 1-3: Very shallow, textbook definitions only
- 0: No engineering understanding shown

### example_quality (0-10)
- 10: Rich, concrete examples with specific scenarios and outcomes
- 7-9: Good examples, somewhat specific
- 4-6: Generic or vague examples
- 1-3: Very poor or irrelevant examples
- 0: No examples provided

### question_alignment (0-10)
- 10: Answer perfectly addresses the question, no deviation
- 7-9: Mostly aligned, minor tangents
- 4-6: Partially addresses the question, significant deviation
- 1-3: Barely addresses the question
- 0: Answer is unrelated to the question

## Voice Dimensions

### fluency (0-10)
- 10: Smooth and natural delivery
- 7-9: Generally fluent with minor hesitations
- 4-6: Frequent pauses or awkward phrasing
- 1-3: Very choppy delivery
- 0: Incomprehensible

### filler_words (0-10)
- 10: Virtually no filler words
- 7-9: Few filler words (1-2 per paragraph)
- 4-6: Noticeable filler words (3-5 per paragraph)
- 1-3: Frequent filler words disrupting flow
- 0: Every sentence has fillers

Note: Score inverted - lower fillers = higher score.

### redundancy (0-10)
- 10: Concise, no repetition
- 7-9: Minor repetition
- 4-6: Noticeable redundancy
- 1-3: Significant repetition of ideas
- 0: Extremely redundant

### spoken_clarity (0-10)
- 10: Crystal clear, easy to follow
- 7-9: Mostly clear
- 4-6: Some unclear or ambiguous statements
- 1-3: Often unclear
- 0: Very difficult to understand

### answer_pacing (0-10)
- 10: Well-paced, balanced coverage
- 7-9: Generally good pacing
- 4-6: Rushed in parts or too slow
- 1-3: Poor pacing throughout
- 0: No sense of pacing
