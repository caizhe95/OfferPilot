# Transcript Voice Rubric

When scoring voice dimensions from text transcripts (without actual audio), use these indicators:

## Fluency Indicators
- Smooth sentence transitions → high fluency
- Abrupt topic changes → low fluency
- Incomplete sentences → low fluency
- Natural connectors → high fluency

## Filler Word Detection
Common Chinese fillers to count:
- 嗯, 呃, 啊, 哦
- 就是, 就是说, 那个, 这个
- 然后 (when overused)
- 对吧, 对不对
- 反正, 基本上

## Redundancy Indicators
- Repeated phrases within short distance
- Same idea expressed with different words (3+ times)
- Unnecessary elaborations
- Circular reasoning

## Spoken Clarity Indicators
- Clear thesis statements
- Logical progression of ideas
- Appropriate technical term usage
- Well-defined scope boundaries

## Answer Pacing Indicators
- Balanced coverage of subtopics
- Appropriate depth per topic
- Time awareness (did they rush key points?)
- Transition quality between topics

## Scoring Note
Voice scores from transcripts are inherently less accurate than from actual audio.
Mark scores with an asterisk (*) when based on text analysis only.
When actual audio is available (Phase 12+), use real acoustic features.
